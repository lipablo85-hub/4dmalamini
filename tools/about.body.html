FAQ

Questions, answered

Is it free?

Yes. No sign-up, no app, no fees. Just open the site.

Will it alert me when my number comes out?

Yes, while the site is open on your phone: your number lights up and a congratulations pops up the moment the result arrives. Keep the tab open on draw night.

Is this the official result?

4D Malam Ini is independent and not linked to any operator. Results are compiled from what the companies publish and cross-checked, but always confirm with the company before claiming.

How far back can I check?

Every draw since November 2021, for Magnum, Da Ma Cai, Sports Toto, GD Lotto and 9 Lotto.

Where are my numbers stored?

Only on your own phone. We never see them.

Who can play 4D?

In Malaysia, only non-Muslims aged 21 and above may buy tickets or claim prizes. Please play responsibly.
